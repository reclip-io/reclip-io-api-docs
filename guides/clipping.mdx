---
title: "Create AI clips"
description: "Turn a source video into short clips."
---

Upload a source for ai_clipper, then quote and submit:

```json
{"tool":"ai_clipper","input":{"asset_id":"YOUR_ASSET_UUID"},"options":{"analysis_start_seconds":0,"analysis_end_seconds":60,"captions_enabled":true,"language":"en"},"maximum_outputs":2,"maximum_credits":100}
```

This operation requires an explicit analysis range in the public service. Source links must satisfy the clipper's supported URL rules.

After success, GET /jobs/\{id\}/clips lists clip IDs. Use GET /clips/\{id\} to read editable captions and the editor revision. Download output assets through the signed download endpoint.
